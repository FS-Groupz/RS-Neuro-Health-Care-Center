Our Healthcare Background

Who We Are & What Drives Our Practice

RS Neuro Health Care Center was established to provide clinical neurological consultations and supportive recovery guidance to individuals experiencing nerve disorders, spinal discomfort, and complex neurological symptoms.

What the center focuses on: Our practice emphasizes systematic clinical examinations, precise diagnostic correlation, and conservative management pathways for headache, spine health, sciatica, and motor recovery.

Patient-centered philosophy: We believe effective neurological care begins with attentive listening. By allowing adequate time for each consultation, our team ensures patients understand their health status and treatment options thoroughly.
